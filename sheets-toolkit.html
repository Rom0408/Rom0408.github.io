Sheets Cleanup & Hebrew Toolkit

A Google Sheets add-on that cleans messy data in one click and adds Hebrew-aware functions. Runs entirely inside your spreadsheet: no server, no data leaves Google.

Cleaning actions

  • Trim spaces and convert text to numbers
  • Remove duplicate rows and empty rows
  • Fill down blanks, split a column by comma, sort
  • UPPERCASE, lowercase, Proper Case
  • Strip Hebrew nikud; set sheet right-to-left or left-to-right

Hebrew functions

  • GREG2HEB(date, "he"|"en") Gregorian date to Hebrew date
  • HEB2GREG(text) Hebrew date to Gregorian date
  • HEBHOLIDAY(date) holiday name (Israel schedule)
  • HEBNUM(n) number to Hebrew numeral
  • GEMATRIA(text), STRIPNIKUD(text)
