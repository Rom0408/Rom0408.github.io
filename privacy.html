Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This policy covers all GridSmith add-ons for Google Sheets, the GridSmith website (rom0408.github.io) and its free browser tools.

What the add-ons access. Only the spreadsheet in which you run them (scope: spreadsheets.currentonly) and the Sheets menu and dialog UI (script.container.ui). They read and write cells only when you choose a menu action or use one of their functions. Spreadsheet content is processed inside Google and is never sent to us or to any third party.

License check. When you enter a license key, the add-on sends that key and the add-on's name to our license server (a Google Apps Script web app, scope: script.external_request) to confirm it is valid, and repeats the check about once a week. Nothing else is sent: no spreadsheet content, no name, no email. The key and the time of the last check are stored in your own Google account's add-on settings (user properties).

What we store. When you buy, we keep a record of your license: the key, the add-on or plan, your email address, the purchase date, its status and the Paddle transaction or subscription ID. We use it only to email your key, validate it, and answer support requests. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Payments. Payments are handled by Paddle.com (Merchant of Record), which collects and stores payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see card details.

Email. We email your license key once after purchase, from sparkyardlabs@gmail.com, and reply when you write to us. No newsletters or marketing email.

Website. The site has no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address under Google's privacy policy. Your light/dark choice is stored in your browser only. The free browser tools run entirely in your browser; text you paste is never uploaded.

Google user data. GridSmith's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion. License records are kept while the license is valid and as required for tax and accounting. To have your record deleted, email us. Uninstall an add-on at any time from Google Workspace Marketplace → Manage apps; this also removes its stored settings.

Children. Not directed at children.

Changes. Updates will be posted at this URL with a new date.
