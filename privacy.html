Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

What the add-on accesses. Only the spreadsheet in which you run it (scope: spreadsheets.currentonly) and the Sheets menu UI. It reads and writes cells only when you choose a menu action or use one of its functions.

What we collect. Nothing. The add-on has no server, no analytics, no cookies, and sends no data to us or to third parties. Spreadsheet content is never stored or transmitted outside Google.

Payments (Pro subscription). Payments are handled by Paddle.com (Merchant of Record), which collects and stores payment details under its own privacy policy. We never see card details; we receive only your email and license status.

Data retention / deletion. We hold no user data. Uninstall the add-on at any time from Google Workspace Marketplace → Manage apps.

Children. Not directed at children.

Changes. Updates will be posted at this URL with a new date.
