Calculate age from a birth date in Google Sheets

Formulas for age in years, and in years, months and days, from a birth date in Google Sheets using DATEDIF and TODAY.

Age looks simple until you need it to be exact. Subtracting two dates and dividing by 365 drifts around birthdays and leap years, so use DATEDIF.

1. Whole years

With the birth date in A2, this returns the completed years as of today.

=DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y")

2. Years, months and days in one cell

Chain three DATEDIF calls with different unit codes.

=DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"Y")&"y "&DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"YM")&"m "&DATEDIF(A2,TODAY(),"MD")&"d"

3. Age on a specific date

Replace TODAY() with a cell that holds the date, such as B2, to get age at a past or future date.

=DATEDIF(A2,B2,"Y")

4. Check a sample with the free calculator

Compare one row with the age calculator to confirm your sheet is reading the dates the way you think.

Watch out for

The MD unit can give surprising results near month ends. For billing or legal cases where days matter, verify the output on a few examples. Dates stored as text will cause errors, so check that the birth dates are real dates.

Frequently asked questions

Why not divide days by 365?

Leap years make a year 366 days long, so the result drifts and can be off by a year near a birthday.
