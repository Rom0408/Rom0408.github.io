Convert numbers to shekel words in Google Sheets

Write amounts such as 120.50 in Hebrew words for checks and invoices, in Google Sheets. What Sheets cannot do, and the options that work.

Checks and some contracts need the amount written out in words. Google Sheets has no function that spells numbers in any language, and Hebrew adds grammatical gender on top.

1. Understand why it is hard

Hebrew number words depend on gender and on the noun: shekel and agora take different forms, and the word for "and" attaches to the last element. A simple lookup table does not cover these rules.

2. For one amount, use the free tool

Type the amount into the shekel-in-words tool and copy the wording into your document.

3. For a column, a function (coming soon)

The planned Israel Business Pack will add SHEKELWORDS. It is not available yet. For 120.5 it is designed to return מאה ועשרים שקלים וחמישים אגורות.

=SHEKELWORDS(A2)

4. Keep the digits next to the words

On a check, write both the digits and the words, and read the words once before printing.

Watch out for

For legal documents, have a person read the result. Also make sure the cell holds a real number and not text with a currency symbol, or the function may not parse it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I do this with a formula and no add-on?

Not reliably. Sheets has no native number-to-words function, and the Hebrew grammar rules are too involved for a short formula.

Does it include agorot?

Yes, the free tool writes the digits after the decimal point as agorot.
