Split full names into first and last name in Google Sheets

Separate first name and last name in Google Sheets with SPLIT and REGEXEXTRACT, and handle the "Last, First" format.

A single name column is awkward for mail merges and lookups. Sheets can split it, but real names are untidy, so decide the rules first.

1. Split on spaces

SPLIT puts each word in its own column.

=SPLIT(A2," ")

2. First and last name only

Take everything before the first space as the first name and the final word as the last name.

=REGEXEXTRACT(A2,"^(\S+)")
=REGEXEXTRACT(A2,"(\S+)$")

3. Handle "Last, First"

When there is a comma, the order is reversed.

=REGEXEXTRACT(A2,"^([^,]+),")
=TRIM(REGEXEXTRACT(A2,",(.*)$"))

4. Review the odd ones

Sort or filter for rows with more than three words, titles such as Dr, and surnames with prefixes, and fix them by hand.

Watch out for

No formula knows which words are middle names. A name like "van der Berg" will be cut in the wrong place by the last-word rule. Check a sample before trusting a whole column.

Frequently asked questions

Can I split Hebrew names too?

Yes. The patterns do not depend on the alphabet.
