Israeli VAT formulas for Google Sheets

Add VAT, strip VAT and find the VAT part of a price in Google Sheets, with the rate in one cell so it is easy to change when the rate changes.

The standard Israeli VAT (ma'am) rate has changed over the years, so a rate typed into every formula is a trap. Put it in one cell and refer to it.

1. Store the rate once

Type the rate as a percentage in a cell, for example F1. The standard rate was raised from 17% to 18% from 1 January 2025. Always confirm the current rate on the Israel Tax Authority site before relying on it.

2. Add VAT to a net price

Multiply by one plus the rate.

=A2*(1+$F$1)

3. Remove VAT from a gross price

Divide by one plus the rate.

=A2/(1+$F$1)

4. Find the VAT part of a gross price

Subtract the net amount from the gross amount.

=A2-A2/(1+$F$1)

5. Handle old invoices with a rate table

List start dates in ascending order in one column and the rate that started on each date beside it. Then look up the rate by invoice date.

=LOOKUP(B2,$H$2:$H$10,$I$2:$I$10)

Watch out for

Fill the rate table from an official source, not from memory, and check rounding rules with your accountant. Some sales are zero-rated or exempt, so a single rate does not fit every line.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I confirm the current VAT rate?

On the website of the Israel Tax Authority. Rates can change, so check it each time you update the sheet.

Should I round each line or the total?

That depends on your invoicing rules. Ask your accountant, then use ROUND consistently.
