Find the Hebrew birthday in Google Sheets

How to get the Gregorian date of a Hebrew birthday for any year in Google Sheets, using the free age calculator or a formula.

A Hebrew birthday is the same day and month of the Hebrew calendar as the birth date. Its date in the Gregorian calendar changes every year, so it cannot be a simple date addition.

1. Find the Hebrew birth date

Convert the birth date to its Hebrew date with the free converter or with GREG2HEB. The day and Hebrew month are what repeat each year.

2. Get the date for a chosen year

In the free age calculator, enter the birth date and the year you want, and read the Gregorian date of the Hebrew birthday.

3. Do it for a list (coming soon)

The planned Israeli Workdays & Dates Pack will add HEBBIRTHDAY, which will take the birth date and a year. It is not available yet; until then, use the free tool for each date.

=HEBBIRTHDAY(A2,2027)

4. Mark the date in a calendar

Paste the dates into a column and import them into a calendar to receive reminders.

Watch out for

The Hebrew calendar has leap years with an extra month, which affects people born in Adar. Customs for the birthday in those cases differ, so check with your family or community. A Hebrew day begins at nightfall, so an evening birth can count as the next Hebrew day.

Frequently asked questions

Can plain Sheets formulas do this?

No. Sheets has no Hebrew calendar functions, so it needs a converter or an add-on.

Is the age the same?

Age in years is normally counted on the Gregorian birthday, which is a separate calculation.
