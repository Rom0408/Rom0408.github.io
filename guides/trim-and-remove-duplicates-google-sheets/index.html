Trim spaces and remove duplicates in Google Sheets

Clean a messy column in Google Sheets: remove extra spaces and non-breaking spaces, drop duplicates and sort, with formulas.

Most lookup failures come from invisible differences: a trailing space, a double space, a non-breaking space pasted from a web page. Clean the text before you compare it.

1. Trim spaces

TRIM removes leading and trailing spaces and reduces repeated spaces inside the text to one.

=TRIM(A2)

2. Handle non-breaking spaces

Web pages often bring character 160, which TRIM does not remove. Replace it with a normal space first.

=TRIM(SUBSTITUTE(CLEAN(A2),CHAR(160)," "))

3. Get a unique, sorted list

Apply UNIQUE to the cleaned range and sort it.

=SORT(UNIQUE(FILTER(C2:C,C2:C<>"")))

4. Or use the free tool

For a one-off, paste the column into the text list cleaner, tick the options and paste it back.

Watch out for

Text that differs only by capital letters may or may not be treated as a duplicate depending on the tool you use, so test on a few rows. Keep your original column before replacing it.
