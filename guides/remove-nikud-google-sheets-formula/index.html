Remove nikud in Google Sheets with a formula

A REGEXREPLACE formula that strips Hebrew vowel points and cantillation marks from a cell or a whole column in Google Sheets.

Pointed Hebrew will not match its unpointed spelling in a lookup, a filter or a duplicate check. Removing the marks first fixes that, and Sheets can do it with one formula.

1. Use REGEXREPLACE with the nikud ranges

The pattern lists the Unicode ranges of cantillation marks and vowel points and replaces them with nothing.

=REGEXREPLACE(A2,"[\x{0591}-\x{05BD}\x{05BF}\x{05C1}\x{05C2}\x{05C4}\x{05C5}\x{05C7}]","")

2. Apply it to a whole column

Wrap it in ARRAYFORMULA in the header row, or fill the formula down.

=ARRAYFORMULA(REGEXREPLACE(A2:A,"[\x{0591}-\x{05BD}\x{05BF}\x{05C1}\x{05C2}\x{05C4}\x{05C5}\x{05C7}]",""))

3. Paste values

Copy the result and paste values only if you want to keep it after deleting the originals.

Watch out for

The pattern deliberately leaves the maqaf (Hebrew hyphen) and sof pasuq in place. Removal cannot be undone from the output, so keep the source column.

Frequently asked questions

Why not use a range from the first to the last Hebrew point?

A single wide range would also remove punctuation such as the maqaf. The listed ranges avoid that.
