Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The add-on has a free tier. Pro is $19 per year with unlimited rows. See the pricing page.

Does it see or store my data?

No. It runs inside your sheet only; nothing is sent anywhere.

Why does =GREG2HEB show an empty cell?

Input must be a real date, not text. Use Clean selection first, or =DATEVALUE().

HEB2GREG returns empty.

Format: day month year, e.g. ט״ו בניסן תשפ״ד or 15 ניסן 5784. In leap years write אדר א / אדר ב.

Which Hebrew dates are after sunset?

Dates change at midnight (no sunset logic).

Does dedupe delete my original data?

It rewrites the selected range with unique rows (Ctrl+Z undoes it). Select only the table you want deduped.

Clean selection changed "00123" to 123.

Text-numbers are converted to numbers; exclude ID/ZIP columns from the selection.

Custom functions slow?

They run per cell; use on ranges via ARRAYFORMULA: =ARRAYFORMULA(GREG2HEB(A2:A)).

How do I uninstall?

Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons.
