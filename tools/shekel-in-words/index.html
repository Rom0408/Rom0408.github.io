How to write an amount in Hebrew words

Checks, contracts and some invoices ask for the amount written out in words as well as digits. Hebrew number words change with gender and context, which makes typing them by hand slow and easy to get wrong.

  1. Type the amount, for example 120.50.
  2. The Hebrew wording appears below, with shekels and agorot handled separately.
  3. Press Copy and paste the text into your document.

Always read the result once before you rely on it, especially for legal documents. The tool is built for common amounts up to the millions. For a column of amounts in a sheet, see the Sheets guide below.

Frequently asked questions

Does it include agorot?

Yes. Digits after the decimal point are written as agorot.

Can it handle large amounts?

It handles thousands and millions.

Is the text sent anywhere?

No. It is generated in your browser.
