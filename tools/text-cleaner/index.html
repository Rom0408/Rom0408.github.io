How to clean a column of text

Lists copied from emails, PDFs and exports usually carry stray spaces, repeated rows and inconsistent capitals. Those details break lookups and counts. This tool fixes the common ones in one pass.

  1. Copy a column from your spreadsheet and paste it into the box.
  2. Tick the clean-ups you need: trim spaces, remove empty lines, remove duplicates, sort A-Z, strip nikud, or set the case.
  3. Copy the cleaned list and paste it back into your sheet.

Work on a copy of your column so you can compare before and after. Removing duplicates keeps one of each item, and sorting changes the order of rows, so do not use it on a column that must stay lined up with other columns.

Frequently asked questions

Will it keep the original order?

Only if you do not tick sort.

What case options are there?

Keep as is, UPPER, lower or Proper case.

Does it work with Hebrew?

Yes. It treats any text as lines, and it can strip nikud too.
