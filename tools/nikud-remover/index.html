How to remove nikud from Hebrew text

Nikud are the small vowel marks written under, over and inside Hebrew letters. They are stored as separate combining characters, so שָׁלוֹם and שלום are different strings to a spreadsheet, a search box or a database. Removing them makes matching and sorting reliable.

  1. Paste your pointed text into the left box. The example text is replaced as soon as you type.
  2. Read the right box. It shows the same words with vowel points and cantillation (trop) marks removed.
  3. Press Copy and paste the result where you need it.

Keep the original text somewhere. Removing nikud cannot be reversed, because the tool has no way to guess which vowels belonged to each letter. If you need the same clean-up for a whole column, the Sheets guide below shows a one-cell formula.

Frequently asked questions

What is nikud?

Nikud are the vowel points and related marks added to Hebrew letters, mostly in children's books, prayer books, poetry and dictionaries. Everyday Hebrew is written without them.

Does the tool change the letters themselves?

No. It removes the marks and leaves letters and spaces as they are.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to a server.
