How to calculate the gematria of a Hebrew word

Gematria assigns a number to every Hebrew letter, so any word or phrase has a numeric value. This calculator uses the standard method, mispar hechrechi: aleph is 1 through tet 9, yod is 10 through tsadi 90, then qof 100, resh 200, shin 300 and tav 400.

  1. Type or paste a Hebrew word or phrase into the box.
  2. Read the total value, and the value of each letter in the breakdown.
  3. Change the text to compare words. Words with the same total are said to share a gematria.

Final letters (sofit forms such as ם and ן) count the same as their regular forms here, and nikud is ignored. Other methods exist, such as separate values for final letters, so a number from another site may differ. Check which method a source uses before comparing.

Frequently asked questions

Which gematria method does this use?

The standard method, mispar hechrechi, where each letter has a fixed value from 1 to 400.

Do spaces and punctuation count?

No. Only Hebrew letters add to the total.

Can I calculate gematria for a whole sheet of words?

Yes. The Sheets guide below shows a formula that works on a column.
