Hebrew Date Converter

Turn any Gregorian date into its Hebrew date, or a Hebrew date into a calendar date. Works for years 1900-2100.

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Accepted Hebrew date formats

  • ט״ו בניסן תשפ״ד letters with the year as a Hebrew numeral
  • 15 ניסן 5784 digits
  • Leap years: write אדר א or אדר ב

Dates change at midnight (no sunset logic). Holidays follow the Israel schedule, major days only.
