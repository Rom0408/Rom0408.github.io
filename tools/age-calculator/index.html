How to find an exact age and the Hebrew birthday

Age in years is simple, but exact ages in years, months and days need care at month ends. The Hebrew birthday adds another twist: it falls on a different calendar date every year.

  1. Enter the birth date.
  2. Read the exact age as of today.
  3. Enter a year in the Hebrew birthday field to see the calendar date of the Hebrew birthday in that year.

The Hebrew birthday is the same Hebrew date as the birth date, so the Gregorian date moves each year. Customs differ for people born in Adar in a leap year, so confirm with your community if the exact day matters.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my Hebrew birthday on a different date each year?

The Hebrew calendar is not aligned to the Gregorian year, so the same Hebrew date lands on a different calendar day.

Can I calculate ages for a whole list?
