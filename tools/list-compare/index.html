How to compare two lists

Comparing two columns by eye is slow and error-prone. Paste both lists here and the tool sorts every item into three groups: only in the first list, only in the second, and in both.

  1. Copy the first column from your sheet and paste it into list A.
  2. Copy the second column into list B.
  3. Read the three results: unique to A, unique to B, and common to both. Copy any group back to your sheet.

Capital letters and extra spaces are ignored, so "Acme Ltd" and " acme ltd " count as the same item. Punctuation still matters, so "Acme Ltd." is different from "Acme Ltd". If near-matches are the problem, clean the lists first.

Frequently asked questions

Is the comparison case sensitive?

No. Case and surrounding spaces are ignored.

How big can the lists be?

Ordinary spreadsheet columns are fine. Everything runs in your browser.

Can I find near-duplicates such as spelling differences?

This tool matches exact text only. Fuzzy matching needs a different approach.
