Planned features

What it will do

This add-on is in development. Everything below is planned and may change before launch.

🔢

Real numbers

PARSENUM will understand currency, brackets for negatives, trailing minus, 12%, 1.5k, 2M and European 1.234,56.

📆

Real dates

PARSEDATE will read 20240229, 5th March 2024, 03/04/24 and more. You will choose day-first or month-first.

Preview · Functions
PARSENUM(text)Messy text to a number
PARSEDATE(text, [DMY|MDY])Many formats to a date
SPLITNAME(name, part)first, middle, last, title, suffix
PROPERNAME(text)Proper case for names
EXTRACT(text, kind, [n])email, url, phone, number, hashtag
🧑

Names, split and fixed

SPLITNAME will pull title, first, middle, last and suffix. PROPERNAME will get McDonald and van der Berg right.

Extract anything

EXTRACT will find the nth email, URL, phone number, number or hashtag inside free text.

Phones, normalised

PHONECLEAN will turn 054-123-4567 into +972541234567 using your default country code.

Private

Will run inside your spreadsheet. Your sheet data won't be sent to us.
